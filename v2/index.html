Voice calibration

Say each word back after you hear it, then confirm whether the app heard it correctly. This teaches the matcher your own pronunciation of the files, digits and piece names — it never replaces the app's normal listening, only helps it recognise you specifically.

Voice commands
  • e4 — pawn moves by square alone
  • knight f3 / knight to f3 — piece + destination
  • queen takes e5 — captures
  • castle kingside / short castle / long castle
  • e8 promote to queen — or just say e8 and it will ask
  • show board / hide board — works any time, even mid-game
  • what's on the board, whose turn, material, how much time, repeat, take back, new game
  • Ask about the position — where are my knights, where can my bishop go, what's on e4, is d5 empty, what's attacking my queen, is my knight defended, anything hanging, am I in check, can I still castle, what did I just play, what were the last three moves, how many pieces do I have, what can I take
  • Ambiguous? It asks "which knight — b or d?" — answer with just b
  • Ask the engine (Coach, on by default — say coach off to silence it) — how am I doing, what should I worry about, is my king safe. Deliberately vague: it won't give you a number or a move unless you set coach full, because this is meant to be training.
  • Puzzles — set Opponent to Puzzles. Mate in one or two, four to seven pieces so the position fits in your head, read out loud when it starts. Say next puzzle, solution, or say the position again. Every puzzle has exactly one key move, so a rejection means it really wasn't the move. No engine and no network needed.
  • Tips (on by default — say tips off) — occasional spoken facts you'd have seen with a board in front of you: both queens coming off, a piece type disappearing, a repeated position, the fifty-move count. Never advice, and never a move — that's what the coach is for.
  • A better voice — set Speech to Natural. It downloads a 326 MB model once (then it's cached and works offline), needs WebGPU, and falls back to the system voice if anything is missing. Nothing downloads until you pick it.
  • Interrupt it — press Esc (or tap the mic) any time it's talking. Talk over it does the same by voice, but it leaves the mic open while the app speaks, so it depends on your machine's echo cancellation — try it, and turn it off if it cuts itself short.
