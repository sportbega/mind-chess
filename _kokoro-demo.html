That’s the whole problem in three rows: the only fast build is the one too big to put in the repo, and the only build that fits takes about three seconds before a move gets announced. Streaming doesn’t rescue it either — a move like “Knight to eff 3” is a single chunk, so short narrations are exactly where the latency lands hardest. Today’s speechSynthesis starts in about 120 ms.

Scratch page, not part of the app — it isn’t committed and nothing here changes Mind Chess. Voices carry Kokoro’s own grades — start with Heart (A) or Bella (A−). The D and F ones are genuinely bad, and q8 makes them worse; that is almost certainly what sounded like another language. Kokoro ships Mandarin, Japanese and Hindi voices, but kokoro-js refuses those ids outright — they were never in the dropdown. Judge it on sound: if Kokoro is clearly worth it, the realistic route is loading fp32 from Hugging Face’s free CDN (cached after the first load) with speechSynthesis as the instant fallback — not hosting it ourselves. Supabase doesn’t help here: the size isn’t the binding constraint, the speed is.
